Privacy notice and Terms of use

These notices apply to the Ganji app for Android, published by Zenity LMT in Kenya. They are the same notices shown inside the app, in English and Swahili.

Privacy notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Ganji is a personal finance app made by Zenity LMT in Kenya. This notice explains, in plain language, what information Ganji uses, where it goes and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Your financial records (profile, assessment answers, budgets, expenses, loans, debts, assets, goals, emergency savings plan, coverage records, asset cash movements and recorded valuations) are stored on this device. Optional reminders use generic text and are scheduled on this device. This version of Ganji has no account sign-in, AI coaching or payments. Cloud backup and financial-record sync are not implemented.

M-Pesa messages (Android)

If you turn on M-Pesa import, Ganji reads the M-Pesa messages in your phone's inbox to list your transactions. The messages are read on your phone and never uploaded. You can turn import off at any time in Settings, or remove the SMS permission in your phone's settings. iPhones don't let apps read messages, so on iPhone Ganji shows example transactions instead.

Crash reports

When crash reporting is configured and enabled, technical error details are sent to Sentry to help fix bugs. Ganji disables default personal-data collection and console-log breadcrumbs, but error messages can still contain information involved in a failure. You can turn crash reporting off in Settings.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics are off unless you choose them in Welcome or Settings. When enabled and configured, Ganji tells Mixpanel which screens you open and which features you use (for example, that a budget was created), without amounts, names, phone numbers or messages. Turning analytics off stops this and clears the anonymous identifier.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act you can: see and copy your data (Settings > Download My Data); correct it (edit your name, or Update my answers); delete it (Settings > Delete my data); object to processing (turn off usage analytics or crash reports); and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Children

Ganji is for adults aged 18 and over.

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy: support@ganjiapp.com. If this notice changes, Ganji will show the new version in the app.

Website waitlist

Joining the waitlist on this website opens a Google Form. The contact details you submit are collected through Google Forms for Ganji early-access updates. Review the form before submitting. For questions or removal requests, contact support@ganjiapp.com. The app data practices above are separate from waitlist signup.

Terms of use

Last updated: 15 November 2026

By using Ganji you agree to these terms. Please read them together with the Privacy notice.

Education, not advice

Ganji helps you understand and plan your money. It gives general financial education only. It is not a licensed financial adviser, and nothing in the app is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check important decisions with a qualified professional.

Fedha Health

The Fedha Health Index is an educational financial wellness planning tool only. It is not a credit score, CRB report, or regulated financial rating. It has no effect on your creditworthiness and is not used by any lender, insurer, or financial institution.

Loans and debts

Loan information shown is user-entered for educational tracking only. Ganji is not a lender or credit reference bureau.

Free plan and Premium

Free allows 2 saved holdings, 2 saved goals separately, 2 saved budgets in total and 2 months of charts. The Fedha Health score will be part of Ganji Premium. Ganji Premium is not available yet, and this version of Ganji takes no payments. Your existing records always stay available for viewing, editing, deletion and export.

Your information

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter. Keep your phone secure, for example with PIN lock in Settings. Your records live only on your phone, so losing or resetting it without a copy (Download My Data) loses them.

Changes and availability

Features may change.

Contact

Questions about these terms: support@ganjiapp.com.

Taarifa ya faragha

Ilisasishwa: 15 Novemba 2026

Ganji ni programu ya fedha binafsi iliyotengenezwa na Zenity LMT nchini Kenya. Taarifa hii inaeleza kwa lugha rahisi ni taarifa gani Ganji inatumia, zinakokwenda na chaguo ulizo nazo.

Kinachobaki kwenye simu yako

Rekodi zako za fedha (wasifu, majibu ya tathmini, bajeti, matumizi, mikopo, madeni, mali, malengo, mpango wa akiba ya dharura, rekodi za bima, michango na utoaji wa mali, na thamani zilizorekodiwa) zinahifadhiwa kwenye kifaa hiki. Vikumbusho vya hiari hutumia maandishi ya jumla na hupangwa kwenye kifaa hiki. Toleo hili la Ganji halina kuingia kwa akaunti, ushauri wa AI wala malipo. Hifadhi ya wingu na usawazishaji wa rekodi za fedha bado havijatekelezwa.

Jumbe za M-Pesa (Android)

Ukiwasha uletaji wa M-Pesa, Ganji husoma jumbe za M-Pesa zilizo kwenye simu yako ili kuorodhesha miamala yako. Jumbe husomwa kwenye simu yako na hazipakiwi popote. Unaweza kuzima uletaji wakati wowote kwenye Mipangilio, au kuondoa ruhusa ya SMS kwenye mipangilio ya simu yako. iPhone haziruhusu programu kusoma jumbe, kwa hiyo kwenye iPhone Ganji huonyesha miamala ya mfano.

Ripoti za hitilafu

Uripoti wa hitilafu ukiwa umesanidiwa na kuwashwa, maelezo ya kiufundi ya hitilafu hutumwa kwa Sentry ili kusaidia kurekebisha matatizo. Ganji huzima ukusanyaji wa kawaida wa taarifa binafsi na rekodi za ujumbe wa programu, lakini ujumbe wa hitilafu unaweza kuwa na taarifa zinazohusiana na tatizo. Unaweza kuzima uripoti huu kwenye Mipangilio.

Takwimu za matumizi

Uchanganuzi wa matumizi umezimwa hadi uuchague kwenye Karibu au Mipangilio. Ukiwashwa na kusanidiwa, Ganji huiambia Mixpanel kurasa unazofungua na vipengele unavyotumia (kwa mfano, kwamba bajeti imeundwa), bila kiasi cha pesa, majina, namba za simu au ujumbe. Kuuzima kunasimamisha utumaji huu na kufuta kitambulisho kisicho na jina.

Haki zako

Chini ya Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Data ya Kenya unaweza: kuona na kunakili data yako (Mipangilio > Pakua Data Yangu); kuirekebisha (hariri jina lako, au Sasisha majibu yangu); kuifuta (Mipangilio > Futa data yangu); kupinga uchakataji (zima takwimu za matumizi au ripoti za hitilafu); na kuondoa idhini yako wakati wowote. Pia unaweza kuwasilisha malalamiko kwa Ofisi ya Kamishna wa Ulinzi wa Data (ODPC).

Watoto

Ganji ni kwa watu wazima wenye umri wa miaka 18 na zaidi.

Mawasiliano na mabadiliko

Maswali kuhusu faragha: support@ganjiapp.com. Taarifa hii ikibadilika, Ganji itaonyesha toleo jipya ndani ya programu.

Orodha ya kusubiri ya tovuti

Kujiunga na orodha ya kusubiri kwenye tovuti hii hufungua Google Form. Maelezo ya mawasiliano unayowasilisha hukusanywa kupitia Google Forms kwa ajili ya taarifa za ufikiaji wa mapema wa Ganji. Kagua fomu kabla ya kuwasilisha. Kwa maswali au maombi ya kuondolewa, wasiliana na support@ganjiapp.com. Taratibu za data za programu zilizo hapo juu ni tofauti na usajili wa orodha ya kusubiri.

Masharti ya matumizi

Ilisasishwa: 15 Novemba 2026

Kwa kutumia Ganji unakubali masharti haya. Tafadhali yasome pamoja na Taarifa ya faragha.

Elimu, si ushauri

Ganji hukusaidia kuelewa na kupanga pesa zako. Hutoa elimu ya jumla ya fedha pekee. Si mshauri wa fedha mwenye leseni, na hakuna kitu ndani ya programu kilicho ushauri wa fedha, uwekezaji, kodi au sheria. Thibitisha maamuzi muhimu na mtaalamu aliyehitimu.

Afya ya Fedha

Fedha Health Index ni zana ya elimu ya ustawi wa kifedha na upangaji pekee. Si alama ya mkopo, ripoti ya CRB, wala kiwango cha kifedha kinachodhibitiwa. Haina athari kwa uwezo wako wa kukopeshwa na haitumiki na mkopeshaji, bima, au taasisi yoyote ya kifedha.

Mikopo na madeni

Taarifa za mikopo zilizoonyeshwa ni za elimu pekee. Ganji si mkopeshaji wala ofisi ya taarifa za mikopo.

Mpango wa bila malipo na Premium

Bila malipo unaweza kuhifadhi mali 2, malengo 2 kando, jumla ya bajeti 2 na chati za miezi 2. Alama ya Fedha Health itakuwa sehemu ya Ganji Premium. Ganji Premium bado haipatikani, na toleo hili la Ganji halipokei malipo. Rekodi zako zilizopo zinaendelea kupatikana kutazamwa, kuhaririwa, kufutwa na kuhamishwa.

Taarifa zako

Unawajibika kwa usahihi wa unachoweka. Linda simu yako, kwa mfano kwa kufuli ya PIN kwenye Mipangilio. Rekodi zako zinakaa kwenye simu yako pekee, kwa hiyo kuipoteza au kuiweka upya bila nakala (Pakua Data Yangu) kutazipoteza.

Vipengele vinaweza kubadilika.
