FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Ganji is preparing for early access. The app's core tools are implemented; public AI access, account sign-in and payment acceptance are still being prepared and tested. Join the waitlist for release updates. A launch date has not been announced.

The selected launch is a direct Android APK plus a web portal for accounts and payments. The portal does not host your financial records. A Play Store release is not part of the current launch plan because SMS access requires a separate distribution review.

Ganji helps you understand and plan your money. It gives general financial education only. It is not a licensed financial adviser, and nothing in the app, including the AI coach, is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check important decisions with a qualified professional.

Savings records an emergency balance, essential expenses and target months, with an optional assumed-return illustration. Goals records target amounts, dates and progress linked to holdings. Ganji does not hold, transfer or lock funds. Free includes three saved goals; Premium removes that cap.

Financial records and chat history stay on your device. Optional AI coaching sends your question, recent chat and permitted aggregate figures through Ganji to OpenRouter and its model provider. Google/Firebase handles account sign-in and Pesapal handles checkout. PIN and biometric unlock are available. Read the privacy notice below for the full details.

Premium's educational 0–100 score uses Savings Rate (30%), Budgeting (20%), Debt Control (20%), Emergency Buffer (20%) and Protection (10%). It uses active transactions and saved inputs; demo transactions can contribute before SMS import and on iOS. The Fedha Health Index is an educational financial wellness planning tool only. It is not a credit score, CRB report, or regulated financial rating. It has no effect on your creditworthiness and is not used by any lender, insurer, or financial institution.

Ganji is for Kenyan adults aged 18 and over, including salaried workers, students, freelancers and business owners tracking their personal finances. You choose what to record and can use English or Swahili.

M-Pesa inbox import is Android-only and requires your permission. iPhone cannot read the SMS inbox, so its current build uses example transactions. Before Android import, and after an inbox read failure, example transactions are also used. Example transactions are excluded from data exports. Manual or pasted transaction entry is on the roadmap.

Settings lets you export a JSON copy and erase local financial records, chat and assessment data. Language and theme preferences and membership/payment recovery are retained. Cloud-account deletion is a separate action; some billing and usage records remain, and deleting or reinstalling does not reset AI allowances. Financial backup and JSON restore are not available yet.
